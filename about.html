About Dough Not Disturb

Welcome to Dough-Not-Disturb! We are a student-led gourmet bakery dedicated to bringing fresh, handcrafted donuts straight to your study sessions and breaks. Our motto is simple: "We bake, you pick up!"

Founded with a passion for unique flavors, every donut is fried to perfection each morning using premium ingredients. From our signature Raspberry Blast to the indulgent Zero Fudge Given, we ensure every bite offers a sweet escape from your busy day.

Pre-order your fresh batch online, skip the long lines, and pick up your hot treats right at our campus hub. Give yourself the break you deserve—because when it's donut time, please Dough-Not-Disturb!
